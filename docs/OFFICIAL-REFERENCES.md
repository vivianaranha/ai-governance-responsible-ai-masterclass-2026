## NIST AI Risk Management Framework

- AI RMF: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1): https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

**2026 note:** NIST states that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. The GenAI Profile page was updated April 8, 2026. Treat NIST material as a living risk-management resource and verify the latest version.

## ISO/IEC 42001:2023

Official overview:
https://www.iso.org/standard/42001

ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an AI Management System (AIMS). The course teaches management-system concepts but does not reproduce copyrighted standard text.

## ISO/IEC 23894:2023

Official overview:
https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html

Provides guidance for integrating AI-specific risk management into organizational activities and functions.

## ISO/IEC 42005:2025

Official overview:
https://www.iso.org/standard/42005

Provides guidance for AI system impact assessments addressing intended and unintended effects on individuals, groups and society throughout the lifecycle.

## European Union AI Act

Article 50 transparency guidance:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/guidelines-transparency-obligations-providers-and-deployers-ai-systems

FAQ:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act

**2026 note:** Article 50 transparency obligations apply from **2 August 2026**. A limited grace period until **2 December 2026** exists for the marking/detection obligation under Article 50(2) for certain systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026. Other AI Act provisions have different timelines and conditions.
