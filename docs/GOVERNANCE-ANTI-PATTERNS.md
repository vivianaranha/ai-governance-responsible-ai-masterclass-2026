# AI Governance Anti-Patterns

**Created by School of AI**

## Governance as a PDF
Policies exist, but no one can show enforcement or evidence.

## One risk tier for everything
Low-risk productivity tools and consequential autonomous agents get identical reviews.

## Model-only governance
The organization ignores prompts, RAG sources, tools, agents, identities and vendors.

## Compliance by framework name
Saying "we follow NIST" without mapping actual controls and evidence.

## Legal interpretation by engineering
Jurisdiction-specific obligations should be resolved with qualified legal/compliance expertise.

## Launch once, approve forever
Material changes require reassessment.

## Vendor trust by contract only
Third parties need due diligence and continuous monitoring.

## Audit evidence assembled at the last minute
Evidence should be generated continuously through the lifecycle.
