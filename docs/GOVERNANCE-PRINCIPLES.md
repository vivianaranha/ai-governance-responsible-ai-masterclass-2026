# AI Governance Principles

**Created by School of AI**

1. Inventory before control.
2. Assign accountable ownership.
3. Scale governance to risk.
4. Separate principles, policies and controls.
5. Make controls testable.
6. Retain evidence.
7. Require stronger challenge for higher impact.
8. Treat third-party AI as part of the system.
9. Govern RAG knowledge and agent tools, not only models.
10. Reassess material changes.
11. Monitor production behavior.
12. Treat incidents and near misses as governance signals.
13. Preserve user oversight and contestability where appropriate.
14. Keep jurisdiction-specific interpretation with qualified experts.
15. Continuously improve the governance system.
