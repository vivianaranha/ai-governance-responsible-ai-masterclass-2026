# Governance Lab 20 — AI Governance & Responsible AI Capstone

**Created by School of AI**

## Scenario

You are part of the AI Governance Office at **Meridian Group**, a multinational enterprise with traditional ML, GenAI, RAG, agentic AI and third-party AI systems.

## Outcome

**Build a complete enterprise AI governance operating system and defend it to an executive review board.**

## Step-by-step

1. Identify the AI system and accountable owner.
2. Document purpose, users and affected populations.
3. Define system boundaries and third parties.
4. Identify at least five risks.
5. Assign inherent risk and target risk tier.
6. Select required controls.
7. Identify control owners.
8. Define test methods and evidence.
9. Identify applicable standards/framework references.
10. Identify jurisdictional/legal questions for qualified review.
11. Decide approve/conditional/remediate/escalate/suspend.
12. Define monitoring metrics.
13. Define material-change triggers.
14. Record residual risk.
15. Prepare a five-minute governance review.

## Required deliverables

- inventory record
- risk assessment
- control mapping
- evidence requirements
- decision record
- reassessment triggers
- executive summary
