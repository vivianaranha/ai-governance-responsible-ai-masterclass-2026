# Instructor Guide

**Created by School of AI**

Teach governance through decisions and evidence.

## Introduce tensions

- Product wants to launch; validation fails one threshold.
- Business wants more agent autonomy; security objects.
- Vendor refuses to provide enough documentation.
- A high-impact system lacks clear appeal mechanisms.
- A RAG system retrieves unauthorized information.
- A model change materially alters outcomes.
- Legal says requirements differ by jurisdiction.
- Audit finds controls documented but untested.

Ask students to decide:
- who owns the decision,
- what risk tier applies,
- what control is required,
- what evidence is sufficient,
- whether to approve, conditionally approve, remediate, suspend or retire.
