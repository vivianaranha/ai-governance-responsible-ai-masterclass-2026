# Assessment Strategy

**Created by School of AI**

## Module
- tutorials and quiz — 20%
- governance lab — 50%
- evidence/control quality — 20%
- review-board communication — 10%

## Course
- module labs — 30%
- portfolio projects — 35%
- final exam — 10%
- capstone — 25%

## Passing recommendation
- final exam >= 70%
- lab average >= 70%
- at least 8 portfolio projects
- capstone >= 75/100
- no unresolved critical governance gap involving unauthorized consequential action, serious data exposure, absent accountable ownership, or unaccepted critical residual risk
