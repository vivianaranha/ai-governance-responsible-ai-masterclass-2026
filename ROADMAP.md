# Course Roadmap

**Created by School of AI**

## 12-week intensive
- Weeks 1–2: governance foundations, inventory, risk and policies
- Weeks 3–4: NIST, ISO 42001, risk/impact assessment, EU readiness
- Weeks 5–6: fairness, transparency, privacy, security
- Weeks 7–8: validation, RAG governance, agentic AI/MCP governance
- Weeks 9–10: vendor governance, lifecycle change, incidents, assurance
- Weeks 11–12: capstone governance operating system

## 24-week part-time
One module per week plus four weeks for case studies, governance portfolio review and capstone.
