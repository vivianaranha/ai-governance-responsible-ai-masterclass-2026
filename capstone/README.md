# Enterprise AI Governance Operating System — Capstone

**Created by School of AI**

## Scenario

You lead AI Governance at **Meridian Group**, a 45,000-employee multinational.

The current environment includes:

- 63 known AI systems
- additional shadow AI usage
- traditional ML
- generative AI
- enterprise RAG
- agentic AI and MCP-connected tools
- 11 major AI vendors
- US and EU operations
- inconsistent review processes
- incomplete ownership records
- weak control evidence
- several production incidents and near misses

## Your mission

Design a governance operating system that supports responsible innovation while creating clear accountability and evidence.

## Required deliverables

1. governance charter
2. AI inventory standard
3. AI intake/registration process
4. AI risk taxonomy
5. risk-tiering model
6. responsible-AI policy
7. control catalog
8. NIST AI RMF mapping
9. AI management-system blueprint
10. AI risk assessment method
11. AI impact-assessment method
12. EU AI Act readiness map
13. fairness review process
14. transparency/explainability standard
15. AI data-governance standard
16. AI security/agentic-security controls
17. model/system validation process
18. RAG governance standard
19. agent/MCP governance standard
20. vendor/supply-chain due diligence
21. lifecycle/material-change rules
22. incident and corrective-action process
23. assurance/evidence architecture
24. executive governance scorecard
25. annual management-review cadence

## Systems to govern

At minimum assess:

### System A — Employee Knowledge Assistant
Enterprise RAG with broad employee access.

### System B — Candidate Screening Assistant
Ranks resumes for recruiter review.

### System C — Refund Operations Agent
Uses tools to investigate and initiate customer refunds.

### System D — Developer Coding Agent
Can access repositories and execute approved development tools.

### System E — Third-Party Customer Chatbot
Externally hosted model/service with customer data interaction.

## Required board scenarios

Your governance board will challenge you with:

1. Candidate-screening fairness test fails.
2. RAG assistant retrieves restricted content.
3. Refund agent initiates a high-value action without approval.
4. Model vendor changes its model without sufficient notice.
5. A third-party MCP server is found vulnerable.
6. A production incident becomes public.
7. EU operations introduce a new transparency requirement.
8. Business leadership asks to waive a high-risk control to meet launch date.
9. Audit finds controls documented but not tested.
10. Board asks whether the company can prove its AI systems are actually governed.

## Final decision framework

For each system, choose:

**Approve / Conditional Approval / Remediate / Escalate / Suspend / Retire**

and support the decision with risk, controls and evidence.
