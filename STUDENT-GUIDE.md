## Governance loop

**Inventory → Assess → Classify → Control → Test → Evidence → Approve → Monitor → Reassess**

For every AI system, be able to answer:

- Who owns it?
- What does it do?
- Who can be affected?
- What data/models/tools does it use?
- What risk tier applies?
- What controls are mandatory?
- Who validates the system?
- What evidence proves control effectiveness?
- What changes trigger reassessment?
- What happens when something goes wrong?

## Portfolio

A strong governance portfolio should include:
- AI inventory
- risk taxonomy
- responsible-AI policy
- NIST mapping
- AI management-system blueprint
- impact assessment
- EU readiness map
- fairness/transparency review
- security/agent governance
- vendor assessment
- incident process
- assurance report
- capstone
